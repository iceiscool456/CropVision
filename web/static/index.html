Crops the disease model knows

Disease detection is limited to these 14 crops (leaf-level). Species identification works for tens of thousands of plants via Pl@ntNet.

Conditions it can detect

Powdery mildew Leaf mold Rust Scab Early & late blight Black rot / Esca Leaf spots (Septoria, Target, Gray) Leaf scorch Bacterial spot Mosaic / Leaf-curl virus Citrus greening Spider mites

The free local model gives a quick healthy/diseased read on these crops; Deep diagnosis (Plant.id) provides accurate, specific disease names with treatment for essentially any plant.
